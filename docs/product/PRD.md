# JustGO product overview

JustGO helps adults build social confidence through short, real-world
challenges. Each challenge is a small social action, such as asking a stranger
for a recommendation at a café or starting a conversation at the gym. The person
tries it, marks it done, and can note how it felt. Over time the app shows their
progress.

## Who it is for

Adults who want more confidence in everyday interactions: conversations,
friendships, groups, social events or dating. JustGO is a practice tool, not
therapy. It never claims to diagnose, treat or measurably change anyone's mental
health.

## How it works

1. **Pick a challenge.** Home shows a deck of cards for the chosen venue: café,
   gym and other everyday places. Swipe left to skip a card and right to take it
   on.
2. **Do it.** The challenge screen shows the instruction and a countdown timer.
   It ends only when the person taps Completed or Give up.
3. **Celebrate.** A short success screen recognizes the effort.
4. **Reflect, if they want.** They pick one of five feelings and can write a
   private note. Both are optional.
5. **See progress.** Progress shows reps, active days, streaks and a calendar.
   Each day opens a list of that day's challenges and feelings.

## What the first release includes

- Six venue decks of Level 1 challenges. The [challenge catalog](CHALLENGES.md)
  holds the cards.
- Guest use with no account. Everything stays on the phone.
- Optional Apple or Google sign-in, which syncs history and reflections to the
  person's JustGO account.
- An optional private iCloud copy, a full data download and import, and controls
  to delete all data or the whole account.
- Paid access through a native iOS subscription, after a free beta.
- iOS first. Android comes later from the same codebase.

## Principles

- **Effort over performance.** No points, speed bonuses, leaderboards or pressure
  to intrude on others. Challenges respect consent, social context and personal
  safety.
- **Private by default.** Writing stays on the phone unless the person signs in,
  turns on iCloud or saves a file. Reflections never go to diagnostics, and go to analytics or AI only with the person's explicit, separately approved permission.
- **Nothing is lost.** A completed challenge is saved on the phone first, and syncing is built so it never duplicates or silently drops one.
- **Simple.** One deck, one timer, one optional reflection. No social feed and no
  sharing.

## Not in the first release

Levels and level progression, reminders, custom dictation, lock-screen timers,
questionnaire onboarding, product analytics, an AI coach, web checkout and
Android. The [implementation plan](../implementation-plan/README.md) tracks the
roadmap.
