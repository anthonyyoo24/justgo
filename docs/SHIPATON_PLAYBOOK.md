# Shipaton 2026 Field Guide

## Build Courage. Ship Early. Prove Momentum.

**Last checked:** August 3, 2026  
**Official deadline:** September 30, 2026 at 11:45 p.m. PDT  
**Source:** [RevenueCat Shipaton 2026 Official Rules](https://revenuecat-shipaton-2026.devpost.com/rules)

## The Winning Mindset

Shipaton is not primarily a prototype contest. It rewards a newly launched, working, monetized app that reaches real users and demonstrates what happened after release.

Our strategy:

> Build one memorable social-confidence loop, publish it early, measure real behavior, and turn every iteration into both a better product and a public story.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Register and submit by **September 30 at 11:45 p.m. PDT**.
- The app must run on iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or Android.
- Unless using the student-only Next Gen exception, the app must be fully published on the App Store, Google Play, or Galaxy Store.
- Its **first public store release** must occur during the July 31–September 30 submission period. An update to a previously released app is not eligible.
- The app must be downloadable in the United States.
- RevenueCat must power at least one in-app or web purchase, or RevenueCat Ads.
- The app must install reliably and work as shown in the submission.
- Judges must receive unrestricted premium access through a free trial or promo code until judging ends on October 13.
- The submission and all integrated assets, libraries, APIs, music, and media must be owned or properly licensed.

## What We Must Submit

- Live App Store URL
- English product description
- Public YouTube or Vimeo demo under two minutes
- Actual on-device footage
- 1024×1024 app icon
- At least one 1179×2556 screenshot without a device frame
- Free trial or judge promo code
- Award-specific descriptions
- Growth metrics and post-launch experiments for Grand Prize consideration
- Public post links and lessons learned for #BuildInPublic

The video must avoid unlicensed music, trademarks, private information, and footage of non-consenting strangers.

## Our Best Award Targets

### 1. RevenueCat Peace Prize — Primary

Show that respectful, progressively harder challenges can help people take meaningful social action.

Evidence to collect:

- Challenges completed
- Repeat usage and streaks
- Self-reported social anxiety and confidence trends
- User testimonials
- Examples of accessibility, consent, and safety-minded design

### 2. RevenueCat Design Award — Primary

Make the daily challenge, completion celebration, XP progression, and anxiety grid visually distinctive. Judges value delightful aesthetics, smooth gestures, thoughtful animation, and innovative experiences.

### 3. HAMM Award — Primary

Use a focused RevenueCat subscription strategy with a polished paywall, clear packaging, and real conversion data. Explain why premium personalization and progression fit the product rather than feeling artificially gated.

### 4. #BuildInPublic — High Opportunity

Audience size does not determine the winner. Judges care about the story, response from the public, lessons learned, and product changes made because of feedback.

Tag public posts with **#Shipaton** and preserve every link.

### 5. OneSignal Keep Them Coming Back — Optional

The daily-challenge habit is a natural fit for supportive reminders. Add OneSignal only after the core app and RevenueCat purchases are stable. Eligibility requires a live app, a deployed campaign, the OneSignal App ID, and an implementation description.

## Build Strategy

### The Core Loop

```text
Choose goals
→ receive a challenge
→ swap difficulty if needed
→ take action
→ rate the experience
→ earn XP and maintain a streak
→ see progress
→ return tomorrow
```

### Ship Scope

- Five onboarding focus areas
- 35–40 curated challenges across five difficulty levels
- One recommended daily challenge, with optional additional challenges
- Same-level, easier, and harder swaps
- Post-challenge anxiety rating and lightweight preference feedback
- Basic XP, levels, four awards, and streaks
- Anxiety grid and weekly social-anxiety/social-confidence check-in
- RevenueCat subscription, trial, paywall, and restore purchase
- Essential product analytics
- Privacy, consent, safety, and App Store-compliant health positioning

Initial personalization should use selected goals, current difficulty, and recent variety. Collect richer signals now; improve the recommendation logic after launch.

## Marketing Engine

### The Creative Hook

> One real-world courage challenge every day—built in public by someone doing the challenges too.

### Before Launch

- Publish a simple waitlist page.
- Recruit 30–50 invite-only beta testers.
- Share three build updates per week.
- Record launch content in advance.
- Contact relevant confidence, communication, friendship, dating, and self-improvement micro-creators.

### After Launch

- Publish one short challenge-oriented video per day across Shorts, Reels, and TikTok.
- Send one weekly progress email.
- Share product changes and the feedback that caused them.
- Post respectfully in relevant communities where self-promotion is permitted.
- Use consenting friends, staged interactions, POV shots, or narration—never unsuspecting strangers.

### Weekly Experiment Rhythm

Run one acquisition experiment and one product experiment each week:

| Week   | Acquisition                   | Product                                   |
| ------ | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Launch | Founder challenge series      | First-challenge difficulty                |
| Week 2 | Micro-creator collaboration   | Onboarding completion                     |
| Week 3 | Transformation stories        | Reminder timing and wording               |
| Week 4 | Best-performing content remix | Paywall presentation and trial conversion |

## Numbers That Matter

Track from the first beta:

- Waitlist signups and source
- Installs
- Onboarding completion
- First challenge started and completed
- Day 1 and Day 7 retention
- Weekly users completing three or more challenges
- Challenge swaps and difficulty choices
- Anxiety and confidence check-in participation
- Trial starts, purchases, conversion, and revenue
- Shares, referrals, testimonials, and community engagement

For every experiment, record:

```text
Hypothesis → change → audience → result → lesson → next action
```

That record becomes the Grand Prize, HAMM, Growth, and #BuildInPublic narrative.

## Two-Minute Demo Story

- **0–15 seconds:** The problem and promise
- **15–45 seconds:** Onboarding and receiving a challenge
- **45–75 seconds:** Completing, rating anxiety, and earning XP
- **75–100 seconds:** Anxiety grid, confidence trend, and progression
- **100–112 seconds:** RevenueCat paywall and premium experience
- **112–120 seconds:** Real traction, user quote, and closing vision

## Final Decision Filter

Before adding anything, ask:

1. Does it strengthen the daily challenge loop?
2. Does it improve activation, retention, revenue, safety, or the submission story?
3. Can it be built and tested without delaying App Store submission?

If the answer is not clearly yes, capture the idea and ship without it.

## Success Definition

By September 30, we want more than a finished app. We want:

- A stable public product
- Real completed challenges
- Paying customers
- Evidence of repeat usage
- A visible build journey
- Measurable improvements made after launch
- A compelling story about helping people act despite social anxiety

That combination gives the app its best chance across Peace, Design, HAMM, #BuildInPublic, and the Grand Prize.
