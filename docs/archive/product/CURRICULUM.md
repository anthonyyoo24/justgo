# JustGO Curriculum Structure

**Status:** Draft  
**Total courses:** 4  
**Total levels:** 16  
**Full curriculum:** 300 reps

## Curriculum progression

All users begin with **SOC 101 — Social Foundations**. After completing it, users may proceed through **SOC 201 — Group Social Foundations** and **DAT 201 — Dating Foundations** in either order or in parallel. **DAT 301 — Group Dating Foundations** builds on both 201-level courses.

```text
SOC 101 — Social Foundations
              |
              |-- SOC 201 — Group Social Foundations --|
              |                                         |-- DAT 301 — Group Dating Foundations
              |-- DAT 201 — Dating Foundations ---------|
```

## SOC 101 — Social Foundations

Shared foundational course for general social and dating-oriented progression.

| Level | Name                | Required reps |
| ----: | ------------------- | ------------: |
|     1 | Social Signals      |            30 |
|     2 | Passing Positivity  |            25 |
|     3 | Supported Exchanges |            20 |
|     4 | Ask and Exit        |            25 |
|     5 | One Follow-Up       |            25 |
|     6 | Short Conversation  |            25 |
|       | **Course total**    |       **150** |

## SOC 201 — Group Social Foundations

| Level | Name                        | Required reps |
| ----: | --------------------------- | ------------: |
|     1 | Entering a Pair             |            15 |
|     2 | Joining a Small Group       |            20 |
|     3 | Joining a Larger Open Group |            15 |
|       | **Course total**            |        **50** |

## DAT 201 — Dating Foundations

Foundational progression for expressing romantic interest in one-to-one interactions.

| Level | Name                        | Required reps |
| ----: | --------------------------- | ------------: |
|     1 | Warm Interest               |            20 |
|     2 | Clear Interest              |            15 |
|     3 | Conversation and Invitation |            15 |
|       | **Course total**            |        **50** |

## DAT 301 — Group Dating Foundations

Progression for expressing romantic interest when the person of interest is part of a group.

| Level | Name                                    | Required reps |
| ----: | --------------------------------------- | ------------: |
|     1 | Interest Within a Pair                  |            15 |
|     2 | Interest Within a Group of Three        |            15 |
|     3 | Interest Within a Group of Four or Five |            10 |

|       | **Course total**                        |        **50** |
