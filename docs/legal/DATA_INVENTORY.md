# JustGO data inventory

The revised Phase 13 implementation was technically reconciled October 21, 2026. [APPROVALS.md](APPROVALS.md) lists the approved Privacy Policy and Terms versions and where they are published. Publishing the public documents does not establish private-journal provider suitability or extended signed-device acceptance. [`gate` issues](https://github.com/anthonyyoo24/justgo/issues?q=label%3Agate) track those.

The candidate offers:

- durable phone journals without server account creation;
- optional managed Apple or Google sign-in with private synchronization of account history and journals;
- a separate, off-by-default private CloudKit choice;
- one Data screen for Export data, Import data and Delete all my data.

Every sign-in adopts the phone's current journal, guest or another account's, into the account that signs in, after the cloud-storage disclosure and without a separate merge choice ([one journal per phone](../implementation-plan/one-person-phone.md)). The phone retires the source collection only after the copy saves. The previous account's server data and iCloud copy stay with that account. A delete-all from another device that the phone has not received yet does not stop the copy, so those local entries are copied too. Entries the target account already deleted are skipped. Deck and venue preferences stay on the phone. Signing out keeps the same journal visible and editable. Returning to the same account synchronizes later edits. Product analytics remains disabled. Paid billing is built behind flags that stay off, and [Billing and Superwall](#billing-and-superwall) records what it will process. Build 4 keeps app reporting off. The reviewed `beta-diagnostics` build 5 is signed, uploaded and in Testing for the existing owner group, and the matching sanitized API reporter is deployed. Exact source-map and native symbols match provider storage. Evidence for the native final envelope and cache, and for live deployed fault delivery, remains open. No legal-view or agreement receipts and no per-account document-version gates are restored.

## Candidate data handling

Settings → Account & data links to **Account** and **Data** and has no separate journal backup or recovery actions.

- **Account** contains Apple and Google login-method switches with provider logos and verified connection state, Log out and a red Delete account. Linking and unlinking refresh verified provider identities, preserve the owner and protect the final usable method. No View guest data row or separate signed-in status paragraph remains.
- **Data** contains iCloud Sync, Export data and Import data with matching up and down tray icons, import preview and confirmation, and Delete all my data.

Both deletion actions use the existing confirmation modal with distinct wording and retry state. Essential local navigation to history, account and data does not depend on fresh paid challenge admission. Cloud operations still require authorization and connectivity.

Download produces the versioned `justgo-data` ZIP. It contains:

- the selected owner and owner kind;
- the export time;
- the complete captured history of accepted completions, plus pending accepted completions;
- all current and preserved alternative journal entries, with historical context;
- the requested Reduce Motion and Haptics preferences.

It excludes credentials, sessions, raw queues, replication operations, aggregate rep and streak state, and unsaved drafts. A signed-in download requires a successful synchronization first. Guest and signed-out downloads are phone snapshots, and the signed-out cloud limitations are disclosed. The file is readable and is not encrypted with an app password. Files and share destinations, including iCloud Drive, are user-controlled and separate from CloudKit and system backup.

Before preview and explicit confirmation, Import validates the bounded ZIP and its schema, CRC, version, ownership, IDs, relationships, dates and context. Import needs no sign-in. Any valid file, guest or from any account, is rebound to the phone's current collection. A signed-out import into an account collection uploads when that account signs back in, or moves with the collection into another account that signs in. Stable completion IDs reconcile without duplicate credit, and existing journal versions that differ stay preserved. A preference import succeeds only after a durable readback. The file is not authentication. Validated imported records may be sent to the signed-in account, to the next account that signs in, or to the enabled iCloud copy. Supported older journal-only files are identified as partial backups. App-owned temporary files are removed after an operation, cancellation or failure. After an interrupted process, they are removed before the next operation. Owned deletion also removes them. User-saved copies remain external.

**Deletion:** Delete all my data removes the selected journal's saved completions and writing from the phone, signed-in JustGO cloud and previously associated app-managed iCloud copies while retaining account identity and login methods. Account-owned deletion requires sign-in. A guest journal with no associated cloud copy can be deleted locally and offline. Durable handling of intent, epoch and generation supports retry and prevents stale automatic re-upload. Delete account additionally revokes sessions, closes the canonical owner, hard-deletes the managed Auth user through retryable server cleanup, permanently closes and purges associated iCloud account collections and clears matching phone credentials. Previously associated iCloud copies still require cleanup when Sync is OFF. Unavailable associated Apple accounts leave cleanup durably pending rather than reporting success. Completed guest-source adoption cannot later resurrect the retired source. Neither action deletes the Apple or Google account or cancels a subscription. User-saved files remain. Disconnected devices are not immediately erased. Retained minimal security ledgers must be applied before reopening an older restored database.

**Device protection:** OS-backed token storage and iOS Data Protection are the baseline, not evidence of a completed device audit. On the signed iPhone, verify:

- actual SQLite, WAL, SHM and temporary-file protection;
- lock behavior;
- backup inclusion and exclusion;
- Keychain token failure and retry;
- Files save, pick, cancel and cleanup;
- Apple and Google native sign-in;
- CloudKit provisioning, account changes, conflicts, quota and purge;
- relaunch.

No additional app encryption or end-to-end encryption guarantee is made.

## Billing and Superwall

Phase 16 adds paid subscriptions. On November 20, 2026, Anthony chose
Superwall's `superwallOnly` event tracking (plan Appendix E). The SDK lives only
in `apps/mobile/src/app-support/billing`, configured with
`eventTrackingBehavior: 'superwallOnly'`, which `superwall.test.ts` pins, and
`justgo/import-boundaries` keeps `expo-superwall` there. It stays off until the
billing flag turns on.

**What Superwall receives.** At `superwallOnly`, SuperwallKit 4.18.3 (through
`expo-superwall` 1.8.0) drops trigger-fire events, user attributes and app
`track` calls, and still sends its own events:

- the app user ID: the JustGO owner ID after `identify(ownerId)` for an account,
  or Superwall's random alias for a guest. Logout calls `reset()`;
- device fields: Superwall's device ID, the identifier for vendor, model, OS and
  app versions, bundle ID, install date, locale, language, region, currency,
  time zone, radio type, interface style, font size, low-power mode and Apple Pay
  availability, plus the connecting IP address;
- subscription state: entitlements, active products, customer info and
  transactions, and paywall view counts;
- paywall and purchase events: open, close, decline, purchase and restore start,
  success and failure, and SDK lifecycle events such as app open and session
  start;
- from Apple, through Superwall's App Store connection, renewals, cancellations
  and refunds for the app's products.

The app passes no journal text, feelings, reflections, email or tokens to
Superwall. The SDK's own privacy manifest declares only purchase history, for app
functionality, not linked and not tracking. JustGO's App Privacy answers still
count the data as linked, because the owner ID is attached.

**No tracking, and the dashboard settings that keep it so.** The SDK sends more
when the Superwall dashboard turns on features the app code does not control.
Keep these off while the Privacy Policy says "we don't share it with ad
networks":

- MMP install attribution (`/api/match`), which posts a device fingerprint and,
  with App Tracking Transparency allowed, the IDFA;
- Apple Search Ads attribution, which posts the AdServices token;
- the Google Ads and Meta conversion integrations. The SDK's `adConsent` option
  defaults to granted, so turning one on would send conversions without a prompt.

Turning any of them on makes Superwall data "used for tracking" in the App
Privacy answers, needs the App Tracking Transparency prompt and changes the
Privacy Policy.

**Retention and deletion.** Superwall keeps data while JustGO uses it. Its
[user deletion](https://superwall.com/docs/dashboard/guides/delete-user-data),
by dashboard or API, holds a request for about 25 days, then deletes the user's
events, attributes, aliases and subscription records. It keeps revenue amounts
and dates with the user ID, device IDs, attributes, IP addresses and user agents
removed. Deletion does not cancel an App Store subscription. P16-11 makes Delete
account ask Superwall to delete that owner, and the Privacy candidate says so,
so the candidate publishes only after P16-11 lands. A guest has no owner ID to
delete by, so its Superwall alias stays until Superwall's own retention ends or
the user writes in. Superwall's compliance contact is compliance@superwall.com.

JustGO's own subscription records are in
[What the API stores](../operations/APPLE_SETUP.md#what-the-api-stores-about-purchases).
They outlive Delete all and Delete account, so a deleted account's purchase
never passes to another owner, and they hold no receipt, payload or journal
content.

**Analytics rule.** The
[analytics rule](../../AGENTS.md#rules-and-what-enforces-them) bans product
analytics and tracking SDKs until Phase 17. Superwall is a billing SDK whose
events go only to Superwall, at `superwallOnly`, so the rule records it as the
one exception, and `scripts/quality/analytics-packages.mjs` does not list it.
Changing the tracking level, or sending app data to Superwall through user
attributes or placement parameters, needs a new privacy review.

Anthony originally selected seven-day backups on Supabase Pro. On October 28 he
replaced that with encrypted nightly backups in Cloudflare R2 on the Free plan.
Phase 15 sets their retention period, and the notices must state it. Supabase
documents seven accessible daily backups on Pro and recommends self-managed

guarantee. See [Supabase backup documentation](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/backups).

**Provider recovery copies.** Supabase Free has no daily-backup entitlement but
can still hold background provider backups, and a paused Free project can be
recovered for up to a year. Supabase DPA §11.2's 30-day export window applies at
agreement termination, and whole-project deletion within 30 days is not
per-account erasure. Neither is a deadline for deleted journal rows. Keep
operator exports access-restricted, with a purpose and an expiry, and reconcile
deletions and revocations before a restored database reopens. For
Washington-covered health data, backups must drop deleted data within six months
after the request is authenticated
([RCW 19.373.040](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.373.040)).
Obtain documented expiry evidence before affected use
([#96](https://github.com/anthonyyoo24/justgo/issues/96)). A visible backup list
or a plan name is not that evidence.

**Security records.** Anthony approved keeping the minimal deletion and
revocation records with no expiry: account and credential identifiers,
timestamps, digests, canonical provider mappings and session revocation markers.
They stop old identity material from reactivating a deleted owner, and they let a
lost-response deletion retry return the same result. They hold no journal
content, and the runtime cannot delete them. Removing a marker could let a
revoked proof create a new empty account, though it would not restore the
deleted journal. Do not remove a marker only because its former screen is gone.
Reassess each marker and managed mapping against its function when those
functions or the credential protocol change. Keeping them serves an engineering
purpose. It is not a finding that permanent retention is proportionate in every
jurisdiction.

## Detail the public notices leave out

Phase 13A PR 5 rewrote the public Privacy Policy and Terms in plain language at Anthony's request (November 15, 2026). The behavior is unchanged. The earlier, longer public text is in Git history (`2026-11-14-us-east`). These facts left the public text and stay true:

- **Provider names and region.** At Anthony's request (November 15, 2026) the public Privacy Policy names only Apple and Google, for sign-in, iCloud Sync and on-device sign-in storage, and describes the rest by role. On November 16, 2026, Anthony chose to keep the host by role too ([Host described by role](SUPABASE_NOTICE_REVIEW.md#host-described-by-role-p15-10)). The providers are Supabase (Auth, PostgreSQL and the `api` Edge Function, in us-east-1, Virginia), Cloudflare R2 (encrypted backups), Sentry (app and server error reports, processed in the United States), a Cloudflare Worker (the legal pages, with the old Vercel copy only for builds that embed its links) and Gmail (the support inbox). [Services and planned additions](#services-and-planned-additions) owns their details, and the [Supabase notice review](SUPABASE_NOTICE_REVIEW.md#us-region-candidate) the region.
- **Sentry reports.** A report can hold a random event identifier, a generic error classification, stack locations, app version and build, OS version and the environment. It excludes request URLs and bodies, screenshots, attachments and interaction history, and session replay, product activity tracking and profiling stay off. Sentry receives the connecting address on delivery, and the report settings keep it out of the stored event. Server reports carry a request identifier, HTTP status, a safe classification and a validated database error code, and exclude raw messages, queries and headers. The [Sentry diagnostic boundary](#sentry-diagnostic-boundary) owns this.
- **Abuse controls** keep protected address hashes, not raw IP addresses ([data handling](#candidate-data-handling)).
- **Backups.** R2 backup objects are scheduled to expire seven days after creation, and deletion normally completes within the next day without a guaranteed deadline. A restore reconciles deletions and revocations before it reopens, stays closed if that cannot be confirmed, may miss changes after the last copy, and signs account users out. [Cloudflare backups](../operations/CLOUDFLARE_BACKUPS.md) owns this.
- **Security records** hold account and credential identifiers, dates and digests, as [Retention decisions](#retention-decisions-for-review) explains. Delete all my data and Delete account keep cleanup pending and retryable when the Apple account or iCloud is unavailable, and both reach app-managed iCloud copies even when iCloud Sync is off.
- **Accounts.** Settings → Account lists verified Apple and Google login methods, and one can be connected or disconnected while one stays. An older installation links its existing protected access to the verified sign-in with no recovery key or transfer code. Changing Apple accounts does not combine unrelated iCloud copies. Reinstalling or restoring a purchase does not sign anyone in. An email supplied by someone else grants no account access.
- **Files.** A signed-in download first syncs and needs a connection, and a signed-out account download may omit newer cloud changes. Older journal-only files restore writing, not a complete history, and cannot recreate a deleted account. Unsaved editor text and unfinished challenges are not in downloads or recovery.
- **Legal reader.** Opening Terms & Privacy or continuing past it records no agreement, legal version or analytics permission.
- **Paid access.** Account and data controls stay reachable when paid challenge access ends.
- **Billing.** The candidate names Superwall but leaves out the device field list, the dashboard features that must stay off, the guest-alias gap and the API table names. [Billing and Superwall](#billing-and-superwall) owns them.
