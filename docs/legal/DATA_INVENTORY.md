## Candidate data handling

Settings → Account & data links to **Account** and **Data** and has no separate journal backup or recovery actions.

- **Account** contains Apple and Google login-method switches with provider logos and verified connection state, Log out and a red Delete account. Linking and unlinking refresh verified provider identities, preserve the owner and protect the final usable method. No View guest data row or separate signed-in status paragraph remains.
- **Data** contains iCloud Sync, Download my data and Import data with matching down and up tray icons, import preview and confirmation, and Delete all my data.

Both deletion actions use the existing confirmation modal with distinct wording and retry state. Essential local navigation to history, account and data does not depend on fresh paid challenge admission. Cloud operations still require authorization and connectivity.

Download produces the versioned `justgo-data` ZIP. It contains:

- the selected owner and owner kind;
- the export time;
- the complete captured history of accepted completions, plus pending accepted completions;
- all current and preserved alternative journal entries, with historical context;
- the requested Reduce Motion and Haptics preferences.

It excludes credentials, sessions, raw queues, replication operations, aggregate rep and streak state, and unsaved drafts. A signed-in download requires a successful synchronization first. Guest and signed-out downloads are phone snapshots, and the signed-out cloud limitations are disclosed. The file is readable and is not encrypted with an app password. Files and share destinations, including iCloud Drive, are user-controlled and separate from CloudKit and system backup.

Before preview and explicit confirmation, Import validates the bounded ZIP and its schema, CRC, version, ownership, IDs, relationships, dates and context. Account files require the same authenticated owner. Guest files may be remapped to the selected collection. Stable completion IDs reconcile without duplicate credit, and existing journal versions that differ stay preserved. A preference import succeeds only after a durable readback. The file is not authentication. Validated imported records may be sent to the signed-in account or to the enabled iCloud copy. Supported older journal-only files are identified as partial backups. App-owned temporary files are removed after an operation, cancellation or failure. After an interrupted process, they are removed before the next operation. Owned deletion also removes them. User-saved copies remain external.

**Deletion:** Delete all my data removes the selected journal's saved completions and writing from the phone, signed-in JustGO cloud and previously associated app-managed iCloud copies while retaining account identity and login methods. Account-owned deletion requires sign-in. A guest journal with no associated cloud copy can be deleted locally and offline. Durable handling of intent, epoch and generation supports retry and prevents stale automatic re-upload. Delete account additionally revokes sessions, closes the canonical owner, hard-deletes the managed Auth user through retryable server cleanup, permanently closes and purges associated iCloud account collections and clears matching phone credentials. Previously associated iCloud copies still require cleanup when Sync is OFF. Unavailable associated Apple accounts leave cleanup durably pending rather than reporting success. Completed guest-source adoption cannot later resurrect the retired source. Neither action deletes the Apple or Google account or cancels a subscription. User-saved files remain. Disconnected devices are not immediately erased. Retained minimal security ledgers must be applied before reopening an older restored database.

**Device protection:** OS-backed token storage and iOS Data Protection are the baseline, not evidence of a completed device audit. On the signed iPhone, verify:

- actual SQLite, WAL, SHM and temporary-file protection;
- lock behavior;
- backup inclusion and exclusion;
- Keychain token failure and retry;
- Files save, pick, cancel and cleanup;
- Apple and Google native sign-in;
- CloudKit provisioning, account changes, conflicts, quota and purge;
- relaunch.

No additional app encryption or end-to-end encryption guarantee is made.

### Provider agreements

- **Vercel.** Its Terms (§11.1) incorporate the DPA, but the DPA's processor
  terms cover only Pro and Enterprise customers. The JustGO team is on Hobby, so
  no processor agreement is established for the API or legal projects. DPA
  Schedule 1 §6.1 restricts sensitive customer data, and buying Pro alone does
  not lift that restriction. Hobby permits only personal, non-commercial use, and
  no published exception covers the free beta of a paid app. Do not admit real
  users while Vercel carries journal traffic on an assumed answer. Retire that
  role or present a verified alternative
  ([#123](https://github.com/anthonyyoo24/justgo/issues/123)). The model-training
  opt-out covers the JustGO projects only. The team-level setting was left
  unchanged.
- **Supabase.** Its Terms (§7(b)) incorporate the DPA dated August 1, 2026, which
  has no Free-plan exclusion. Accepting the agreement counts as signing the
  applicable SCCs (DPA §12.2), so a missing separate signature is not a gap. DPA
  §4 allows sensitive data only with lawful handling, notice and any required
  explicit consent, and the current sign-in notice is not shown to meet that bar
  ([#95](https://github.com/anthonyyoo24/justgo/issues/95)). HIPAA protected
  health information would need a separate BAA. An ordinary consumer journal is
  not HIPAA data by default. Canada Central database placement does not make all
  processing Canadian, because support, network and recovery copies have their
  own scope.

These provider facts do not establish a current production configuration. They do not authorize contact, upgrades, agreement acceptance, rollout or publication. JustGO's project-specific model-training opt-out was verified earlier. Do not treat it as proof of every provider payload and system-data exclusion.

documents seven accessible daily backups on Pro and recommends self-managed

guarantee. See [Supabase backup documentation](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/backups).

**Provider recovery copies.** Supabase Free has no daily-backup entitlement but
can still hold background provider backups, and a paused Free project can be
recovered for up to a year. Supabase DPA §11.2's 30-day export window applies at
agreement termination, and whole-project deletion within 30 days is not
per-account erasure. Neither is a deadline for deleted journal rows. Keep
operator exports access-restricted, with a purpose and an expiry, and reconcile
deletions and revocations before a restored database reopens. For
Washington-covered health data, backups must drop deleted data within six months
after the request is authenticated
([RCW 19.373.040](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.373.040)).
Obtain documented expiry evidence before affected use
([#96](https://github.com/anthonyyoo24/justgo/issues/96)). A visible backup list
or a plan name is not that evidence.

**Security records.** Anthony approved keeping the minimal deletion and
revocation records with no expiry: account and credential identifiers,
timestamps, digests, canonical provider mappings and session revocation markers.
They stop old identity material from reactivating a deleted owner, and they let a
lost-response deletion retry return the same result. They hold no journal
content, and the runtime cannot delete them. Removing a marker could let a
revoked proof create a new empty account, though it would not restore the
deleted journal. Do not remove a marker only because its former screen is gone.
Reassess each marker and managed mapping against its function when those
functions or the credential protocol change. Keeping them serves an engineering
purpose. It is not a finding that permanent retention is proportionate in every
jurisdiction.
