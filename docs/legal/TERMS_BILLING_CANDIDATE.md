## Your account

You can use JustGO without signing in, and your journal stays on your phone. You can sign in with Apple or Google to sync it. When you sign in, the journal on this phone is saved to that account. Signing out keeps your journal on this phone.

Keep your phone and your Apple or Google account secure. Don’t use someone else’s account or interfere with the service’s security.

## Subscriptions

Starting a challenge needs a JustGO subscription. Without one, you can still open the app, read and export your journal, change Settings, sign in or out, and delete your data or account.

**Plans and price.** JustGO sells auto-renewing subscriptions through Apple’s App Store. Before you subscribe, the subscription screen shows each plan’s name, length and price, and whether it includes a free trial. Apple shows the same details before you confirm. Prices are in your App Store currency and may include tax.

**Payment.** Apple charges the payment method on your Apple account when you confirm the purchase or, for a plan with a free trial, when the trial ends. JustGO never receives your payment details.

**Free trial.** A plan with a free trial costs nothing during the trial. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription continues and Apple charges the plan’s price. Apple decides who can get a trial, and each Apple account can usually get it only once. If you buy a different plan during a trial, any unused part of the trial may end.

**Renewal.** Your subscription renews automatically for the same length, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple charges each renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.

**Cancelling.** You can cancel at any time in your Apple account’s subscription settings. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. JustGO’s Delete account screen also links there. After you cancel, you keep your subscription until the end of the period or free trial you’re in, and you aren’t charged again. Deleting the app, your journal or your JustGO account does not cancel a subscription.

**Refunds.** Apple handles all payments, so refunds go through Apple under Apple’s policies. You can ask for one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/). We can’t issue refunds ourselves. If Apple refunds the period you’re in, your subscription ends for that period. A refund of an earlier period doesn’t end the period you’re in now. Nothing in these terms limits refund rights you have under the law.

**Price changes.** We may change subscription prices. Apple tells you before a higher price applies to your subscription and, where Apple’s rules or the law require, asks you to agree first. If you don’t agree, your subscription ends at the end of the period you’ve paid for. A new price never applies to a period you’ve already paid for.

**Checking your subscription.** JustGO checks each purchase with Apple before it unlocks challenges. When your saved subscription period ends, the app needs an internet connection to confirm a renewal before you can start another challenge.

**When a subscription ends.** If your subscription ends, is refunded or isn’t renewed, you can’t start new challenges until you subscribe again. Your history, journal and reflections stay, and you can still export or delete them. Activity you recorded while subscribed still syncs to your account.

**Subscribing without signing in.** You can subscribe without signing in. The subscription belongs to the Apple account you bought it with, and your journal stays on this phone. If you later sign in with Apple or Google, the subscription moves into that JustGO account along with the journal on this phone. Once a subscription is in a signed-in account, it stays with that account, and another JustGO account can’t use it. If you reinstall JustGO or move to a new iPhone, you can restore a subscription bought with the same Apple account. Restoring a subscription doesn’t bring back a journal that wasn’t signed in, synced with iCloud or exported.

## Your content

Your journal and reflections remain yours. You allow JustGO to process them to save, show, sync, export, import and delete them as you ask, on your phone and through the account or iCloud Sync you choose. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) explains how.

## Your data

- **iCloud Sync** is optional and off by default. It keeps a private copy in your Apple account.
- **Export data** creates a restorable snapshot of your history, journal and preferences. The file isn’t password protected, so keep it somewhere safe.
- **Import data:** You can import a JustGO backup file without signing in. Its entries are added to the journal on this phone.
- **Delete all my data** removes your journal from this phone, from your account when you’re signed in, and from the app’s iCloud copy. Your account stays open.
- **Delete account** closes your account and removes its journal from our servers, this phone and the app’s iCloud copy. It does not delete your Apple or Google account or cancel an Apple subscription.

Files you saved elsewhere stay under your control. Anything that wasn’t synced or saved in a file can be lost if you lose your phone, remove the app or clear its data.

## Availability

Saving and syncing depend on your network, device and our providers. We may change or interrupt the service for security, maintenance or operational reasons, and we will give any notice the law requires.
