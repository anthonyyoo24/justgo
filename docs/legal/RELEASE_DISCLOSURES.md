## Signed-build and paid-stage gates

Phase 13 must verify privacy manifests/required-reason APIs for the actual installed SDK versions, required declarations, secure hosted connections, no unauthorized collection, accessibility/large text/VoiceOver, anonymous legal URLs from the iPhone and final App Store answers. The local web/iOS exports are not native evidence.

For Sentry, verify signed crash/restart payloads including envelope headers, original local cache/offline handling, IP setting, source maps/dSYMs and provider failure/recovery. Lawful basis, provider agreement and actual trial/retention must be resolved first in Phase 12; ordinary delivery remains off meanwhile.

Before paid launch, 07A/13 update notice/Terms/labels for Apple purchases, RevenueCat and any activated billing transport, plus actual offer/renewal/cancellation/restore and account-deletion explanation. Before post-launch analytics, Phase 14 reviews the provider, exact fields, lawful basis and any required permission, updates notices, and validates privacy requests, retention and no historical backfill. No account-owned legal receipt or version gate is assumed or authorized.
